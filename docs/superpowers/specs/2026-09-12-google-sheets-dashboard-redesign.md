# Design Document: Google Sheets Dashboard Redesign ("Scenario Spotlight")

**Date**: 2026-09-12  
**Status**: Approved (Incorporated Architecture Review Feedback)  
**Topic**: Google Sheets Executive Summary Layout Redesign  

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation

The Google Sheets dashboard generated by `engine/templates/google_apps_script.template.js` exhibits three key UX issues:
1. **Disconnected KPI Summary Cards**: The 5 summary cards at the top (Rows 4–6) were hardcoded with formulas that strictly queried `30y` and `Top 5`. Selecting a different **Horizon** (`10y`, `20y`) or **Strategy** (`Top 3`, `Top 10`) left the scorecards unchanged, confusing users.
2. **Stranded Metric Glossary at Row 80**: To prevent `#SPILL!` errors from the dynamic `FILTER()` formula (which could spill up to 68 rows), the glossary and methodology cards were positioned starting at row 80. When users filtered down to a handful of rows, dozens of blank rows appeared, leaving definitions stranded out of sight.
3. **Data Overload & Intimidation**: Default settings rendered up to 68 rows of dense 14-column tables comparing all strategies, universes, frequencies, and horizons at once.

---

## 2. Solution: "Scenario Spotlight" Layout (Option A)

Transform the Executive Summary from a filter-down dump of 68 rows into a **Scenario Spotlight**:
- The user chooses a single specific scenario using 6 explicit dropdowns + seed capital.
- The 5 KPI scorecards dynamically calculate for the selected scenario vs benchmark.
- A concise **3-row Head-to-Head comparison table** renders the Selected Strategy, the Selected Benchmark, and a live **Net Advantage / Delta** row.
- The **Glossary and Methodology** are positioned immediately below the table (Row 14), fully visible above the fold or with minimal scroll, completely free from `#SPILL!` concerns.

---

## 3. Detailed Layout Architecture

### 3.1 Banner Header (Row 1)
- Merged range `A1:N1` (14 columns).
- Background `#1B365D` (Navy), White bold text: `S&P 500 & ALL-WORLD TOP N STRATEGY — EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD`.
- Row height: 40px.

### 3.2 Parameter Controls Bar (Row 2)
14 columns organized into 7 controls with yellow input cells (`#FEFCBF`) and strict data validation:
1. **Universe** (Cols A–B): Label in `A2` ("Universe:"), dropdown in `B2`: `['S&P 500', 'All World']`. Default: `'S&P 500'`.
2. **Strategy** (Cols C–D): Label in `C2` ("Strategy:"), dropdown in `D2`: `['Top 3', 'Top 5', 'Top 10']`. Default: `'Top 5'`.
3. **Horizon** (Cols E–F): Label in `E2` ("Horizon:"), dropdown in `F2`: `['10y', '20y', '30y']`. Default: `'30y'`.
4. **Rebalance** (Cols G–H): Label in `G2` ("Rebalance:"), dropdown in `H2`: `['Annual', 'Quarterly']`. Default: `'Annual'`.
5. **Benchmark** (Cols I–J): Label in `I2` ("Benchmark:"), dropdown in `J2`: `['S&P 500', 'MSCI World']`. Default: `'S&P 500'`.
6. **Tax Rate** (Cols K–L): Label in `K2` ("Tax Rate:"), dropdown in `L2`: `['0.0%', '15.0%', '20.0%', '30.0%', '37.0%']`. Default: `0.30` formatted as `0.0%`.
7. **Seed Capital** (Cols M–N): Label in `M2` ("Seed Capital:"), input in `N2`: number `> 0`. Default: `BASE_INITIAL_CAPITAL` ($10,000) formatted as `$#,##0`.

*Note: The Seed Capital location remains `$N$2`, preserving full compatibility with `SCALE_EXPR` throughout `Scenario Data` and all downstream tabs.*

### 3.3 Dynamic KPI Scorecards (Rows 4–6)
All 5 scorecards query `'Scenario Data'` directly using deterministic `INDEX/MATCH` lookups based on the active dropdown selections.

**String-Resilient Tax Rate Expression**:  
`TAX_STR = IF(ISNUMBER($L$2), TEXT($L$2, "0.0%"), $L$2)`

**Lookup Keys in `'Scenario Data'!$A:$A`**:
- **Strategy Lookup Key**:  
  `$F$2 & "_" & $B$2 & "_" & $D$2 & "_" & $H$2 & "_" & IF(ISNUMBER($L$2), TEXT($L$2, "0.0%"), $L$2)`
- **Benchmark Lookup Key**:  
  `$F$2 & "_" & IF($J$2="MSCI World","All World_MSCI World","S&P 500_S&P 500") & "_" & $H$2 & "_" & IF(ISNUMBER($L$2), TEXT($L$2, "0.0%"), $L$2)`

#### Card 1: Strategy Final Wealth (Cols A–C)
- **Background**: Soft Mint (`#E6FFFA`), Border `#B2F5EA`.
- **Title (Row 4)**: `=$D$2 & " Final Wealth (" & $F$2 & ")"`
- **Value (Row 5)**: `=IFERROR(INDEX('Scenario Data'!$K:$K, MATCH(<StrategyKey>, 'Scenario Data'!$A:$A, 0)), 0)` (Format: `$#,##0.00`, Font: 14pt bold `#22543D`).
- **Subtitle (Row 6)**: `="After all taxes (" & TEXT($N$2, "$#,##0") & " start)"`

#### Card 2: Benchmark Wealth (Cols D–F)
- **Background**: Slate (`#EDF2F7`), Border `#CBD5E0`.
- **Title (Row 4)**: `=$J$2 & " Wealth (" & $F$2 & ")"`
- **Value (Row 5)**: `=IFERROR(INDEX('Scenario Data'!$K:$K, MATCH(<BenchmarkKey>, 'Scenario Data'!$A:$A, 0)), 0)` (Format: `$#,##0.00`, Font: 14pt bold `#4A5568`).
- **Subtitle (Row 6)**: `="Passive buy & hold"`

#### Card 3: Post-Liquidation CAGR (Cols G–H)
- **Background**: Ice Blue (`#EBF8FF`), Border `#BEE3F8`.
- **Title (Row 4)**: `="Post-Liq CAGR (" & $D$2 & ")"`
- **Value (Row 5)**: `=IFERROR(INDEX('Scenario Data'!$I:$I, MATCH(<StrategyKey>, 'Scenario Data'!$A:$A, 0)), 0)` (Format: `0.00%`, Font: 14pt bold `#1B365D`).
- **Subtitle (Row 6)**: `="Net after-tax annual rate"`

#### Card 4: Annual Alpha vs Benchmark (Cols I–K)
- **Background**: Forest Green (`#F0FFF4`), Border `#C6F6D5`.
- **Title (Row 4)**: `="Annual Alpha vs " & $J$2`
- **Value (Row 5)**: Strategy Post-Liq CAGR minus Benchmark Post-Liq CAGR:  
  `=(IFERROR(INDEX('Scenario Data'!$I:$I, MATCH(<StrategyKey>, 'Scenario Data'!$A:$A, 0)), 0) - IFERROR(INDEX('Scenario Data'!$I:$I, MATCH(<BenchmarkKey>, 'Scenario Data'!$A:$A, 0)), 0))`  
  (Format: `+0.00%;-0.00%;0.00%`, Font: 14pt bold `#22543D`).
- **Subtitle (Row 6)**: `="Excess annual compound return"`

#### Card 5: Annual Tax Drag (Cols L–N)
- **Background**: Coral (`#FFF5F5`), Border `#FED7D7`.
- **Title (Row 4)**: `="Annual Tax Drag (" & $D$2 & ")"`
- **Value (Row 5)**: `=IFERROR(INDEX('Scenario Data'!$O:$O, MATCH(<StrategyKey>, 'Scenario Data'!$A:$A, 0)), 0)` (Format: `0.00%`, Font: 14pt bold `#9B2C2C`).
- **Subtitle (Row 6)**: `="Annual return lost to taxes"`

### 3.4 Head-to-Head Comparison Table (Rows 8–12)

#### Section Header (Row 8)
- Merged `A8:N8`: `HEAD-TO-HEAD PERFORMANCE & TAX SPOTLIGHT` (Background `#1B365D`, Font: 11pt bold white).

#### Table Header (Row 9)
14 Column Headers:
1. `Role / Selection` (Col A)
2. `Universe` (Col B)
3. `Horizon` (Col C)
4. `Frequency` (Col D)
5. `Annual Return (Pre-Tax)` (Col E)
6. `Annual Return (After-Tax)` (Col F)
7. `Annual Return (Post-Liq)` (Col G)
8. `Total Return (Cumulative)` (Col H)
9. `Ending Wealth` (Col I, Formula: `="Ending Wealth (" & TEXT($N$2, "$#,##0") & " Start)"`)
10. `Total Dividends Received` (Col J)
11. `Max Drawdown (Worst Drop)` (Col K)
12. `Total Taxes Paid` (Col L)
13. `Annual Tax Drag` (Col M)
14. `Excess vs Benchmark (Alpha)` (Col N)

| **Col F** | Annual Return (After-Tax) | Column H (`$H:$H`) | `0.00%` |

#### Row 10: Selected Strategy Row
- `A10`: `="★ Strategy: " & $D$2`
- `B10`: `=$B$2`
- `C10`: `=$F$2`
- `D10`: `=$H$2`

- `N10`: `=(G10 - G11)` (True alpha vs selected benchmark)
- Styling: Light green highlight (`#F0FFF4`), bold text.

#### Row 11: Benchmark Row
- `A11`: `="Benchmark: " & $J$2 & " Total Return"`
- `B11`: `=IF($J$2="MSCI World", "All World", "S&P 500")`
- `C11`: `=$F$2`
- `D11`: `="—"`
- `E11:M11`: `=INDEX('Scenario Data'!$<SOURCE_COL>:$<SOURCE_COL>, MATCH(<BenchmarkKey>, 'Scenario Data'!$A:$A, 0))`
- `N11`: `="—"`
- Styling: Muted slate (`#EDF2F7`), italic text.

#### Row 12: Net Advantage / Delta Row
- `A12`: `="Net Advantage (Strategy vs " & $J$2 & ")"`
- `B12:D12`: `"—"`

- `F12`: `=(F10 - F11)` (Format: `+0.00%;-0.00%;0.00%`)
- `G12`: `=(G10 - G11)` (Format: `+0.00%;-0.00%;0.00%`)

- Styling: Warm gold highlight (`#FEFCBF`), bold border.

### 3.5 Metric Definitions & Methodology (Rows 14–26)

Because the table uses fixed Rows 10–12 with zero spill formulas, the glossary is positioned at Row 14:
- **Row 14**: Header `KEY METRIC DEFINITIONS & GLOSSARY` (Merged `A14:N14`, `#EDF2F7`, `#2D3748` bold, height 24px).
- **Rows 15–18**: 2-Column Definition Grid (height 22px each):
  - *Row 15*: `A15:B15` `Annual Return (CAGR):` / `C15:G15` description. `H15:I15` `Annual Alpha:` / `J15:N15` description.
  - *Row 16*: `A16:B16` `Total Return (Cumulative):` / `C16:G16` description. `H16:I16` `Annual Tax Drag:` / `J16:N16` description.
  - *Row 17*: `A17:B17` `Post-Liquidation Return:` / `C17:G17` description. `H17:I17` `Max Drawdown:` / `J17:N17` description.
  - *Row 18*: `A18:B18` `Ending Wealth:` / `C18:G18` description. `H18:I18` `Cash Dividends & Pooling:` / `J18:N18` description.
- **Row 20**: Header `METHODOLOGY NOTE — REBALANCING, TAXES & DIVIDENDS` (Merged `A20:N20`, `#2D3748`, white text, height 24px).
- **Rows 21–25**: Bullet points describing factsheet weighting, drift mechanics, discrete dividend timing, FIFO lot tracking, and self-financing invariant (merged `A:N`, height 20px each).
